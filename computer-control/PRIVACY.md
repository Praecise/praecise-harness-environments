# Privacy

What Praecise Control reads, what it keeps, where, and how to remove it.

## On your computer

Praecise Control reads the windows of the apps you named, and only when an agent you
connected asks. It reads no other app, and it lists no other app to an agent.

- **What is read** is passed to the agent that asked and is not kept by Praecise
  Control.
- **Pictures and recordings** of a window are written to a folder on your computer.
  Recordings are removed after a day and are held to a size limit. Nothing here sends
  them anywhere; the agent that asked for one is given it.
- **While a window is being pictured or recorded** on a Mac, the Praecise Control icon
  in the menu bar turns red and its menu says so. The Windows and Linux apps do not
  show this sign yet.
- **Each app starts in a mode where it can only be looked at.** You change that, per
  app, and anything that sends, pays or deletes waits for you.
- **Pause** in the menu refuses every agent until you resume. **Disconnect** ends the
  agents connected now. **Clear** removes what is stored.

The app does not contact Praecise by itself. It has no analytics and no advertising,
and it does not track what you do.

## The browser extensions

An extension does nothing until you press its button on a tab. It then lets the agent
you connected read and work that one tab, on that tab's site, until you stop it, the
tab closes or it leaves the site. It does not read your other tabs or your history,
and it does not collect or send anything to Praecise.

## The public route

The public route is off until you start it. It lets an assistant or an app you allow
with your Praecise account reach your computer from somewhere else, through a relay
run by Praecise.

- **Requests and answers pass through the relay and are not kept there**: nothing
  read, typed or pictured is written.
- **The relay keeps** the list of your computers (the name, the kind of system, when
  each was last connected), what you narrowed or withdrew for an app on one, and the
  latest 200 things asked of each computer: when, by which app, which tool and action,
  and on which of your apps. It is kept so that you can read it.
- **Your Praecise account** says who you are to the relay. An app is given only what
  you allowed on the consent page, for the computers you named, and until the time you
  set if you set one.
- **You remove** a computer, or withdraw an app from one, at any time; it holds at
  once.
